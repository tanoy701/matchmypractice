Common questions

Do I need to change my current website or software?

Usually not. We connect to what you already use for your site, calendar and email, and recommend changes only where they clearly help.

Will the AI talk to clients without my approval?

Only within the rules you approve. You review the scripts and screening logic before launch, and clinical or sensitive topics hand off to you or your staff.

Is this HIPAA compliant?

Compliance depends on your practice and the tools involved. We design workflows with that in mind and will tell you plainly what needs a BAA or legal review. This page is not legal advice.
